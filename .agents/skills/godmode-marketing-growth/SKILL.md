## Step-by-Step Growth Engine Workflow

1. **Product Marketing Foundation:** Establish the Product Marketing Document — ICP (Ideal Customer Profile), key pain points, core positioning statement, differentiation matrix, and brand tone.
2. **CRO & Copywriting Pass:** Structure high-converting landing pages using PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution) or AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action) frameworks. Optimize CTAs, hero copy, social proof, and benefit bullets.
3. **AI-SEO & Schema Engineering:** Implement structured JSON-LD schemas (Organization, Product, FAQPage, Article). Optimize content for LLM citation and AI search engines (SearchGPT, Perplexity, Claude).
4. **Onboarding & Paywall Optimization:** Reduce time-to-first-value (TTFV) in signup flows. Design clear feature gates, upsell modals, upgrade paywalls, and retention cancellation save flows.
5. **Growth Loop Implementation:** Engineer self-reinforcing viral loops (referral invites, shareable badges, free utility tools, co-marketing integrations).

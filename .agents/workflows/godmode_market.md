# GODMODE Autonomous Marketing Workflow (`/godmode-market`)

> **Goal:** Develop product marketing foundations, CRO landing pages, AI-SEO optimization, and viral growth loops.

---

## Stage 1: Product Marketing Document
- Define Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), core pain points, positioning statement, and value propositions.

## Stage 2: CRO & Copywriting Overhaul
- Write high-converting landing page copy using PAS or AIDA frameworks.
- Optimize hero section, value claims, social proof, and primary CTAs.

## Stage 3: AI-SEO & Schema Integration
- Implement JSON-LD schemas (Organization, Product, FAQPage).
- Optimize page structure for LLM citation and AI search engine visibility.

## Stage 4: Onboarding, Paywalls & Growth Loops
- Streamline signup flow to reduce time-to-first-value (TTFV).
- Implement feature gate paywalls, referral loops, and co-marketing mechanics.

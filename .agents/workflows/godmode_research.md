# GODMODE Autonomous Research Workflow (`/godmode-research`)

> **Goal:** Execute comprehensive, multi-source deep research on any topic, technology, or codebase.

---

## Stage 1: Scope & Query Formulation
- Formulate core research questions and allowlisted domains.
- Construct initial search queries across web, academic papers, and GitHub repositories.

## Stage 2: Recursive Search & Crawling
- Execute web searches and document fetches.
- Parse source code, abstracts, technical documentation, and benchmarks.

## Stage 3: Intelligence Synthesis
- Deduplicate findings and extract key trends, patterns, and empirical evidence.
- Build comparison matrices and trade-off summaries.

## Stage 4: Citation Audit & Delivery
- Verify all claims, statistics, and code snippets against cited source URLs.
- Deliver structured intelligence report.
